# Advanced Vehicles

## Commands
* `/park` - Parks your vehicle on the street persistently.
* `/vehcontrol` - Opens the vehicle interaction menu (Doors, Windows, Trunk, Hood).
* `/givekey` - Opens a menu to duplicate your vehicle keys for another player.
* `/repo` - (Job Restricted) Opens the repossession target locator.
* `/repo_execute` - (Job Restricted) Repossesses the vehicle you are standing next to.
* `/admingivecar [id] [model]` - (Admin Only) Spawns and registers a vehicle permanently to a player.
* `/fixui` - Forcefully closes any stuck menus and restores your game controls.

## Keybinds
* **G (Keyboard) / D-Pad Right (Controller)** - Toggle Engine
* **L (Keyboard) / D-Pad Left (Controller)** - Toggle Locks
* **H (Keyboard) / RB + A (Controller)** - Start Hotwire Minigame
* **B (Keyboard) / D-Pad Down (Controller)** - Toggle Seatbelt
